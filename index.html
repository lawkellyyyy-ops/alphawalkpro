Everything we get asked in the first ten minutes. If yours isn't here, ask Alpha in the chat.

Does Alpha place trades for me?
No. Alpha never touches the trigger. The crew reads, reasons, and pings you. The tap is always yours. Paper trades only during beta — no money moves on our side.
What does it cost?
Free during beta. No card to start. When we open paid plans alongside live trading, the price is $49 / month — and beta walkers keep that price for life. No surprise tiers, no usage meters.
Is this investment advice?
No. alphawalk is software for research and trade-craft. Not investment advice. It does not constitute a recommendation or an offer to buy or sell any security. Trading involves risk of loss. Past performance does not indicate future results.
How is setup different from other AI trading tools?
Thirty seconds inside Telegram. Tell the crew your markets and your risk tolerance once. No dashboards. No API keys. No afternoons lost to onboarding.
What does alphawalk actually do for me?
Your crew watches the markets you care about and explains setups like a patient mentor — voice or text, only when it matches your cadence. Over time you stop collecting signals and start reading the market for yourself. That's the walk.
Where does my data live?
Inside the messenger you already use. We never sell, share, or repackage walker data. Conversations are end-to-end encrypted; you can delete your history at any time and we wipe ours alongside.
Why alphawalk?
Markets can't be reliably predicted from their past — the random walk hypothesis, made famous by Burton Malkiel in 1973. We took that seriously. If the next step is unknowable, the answer isn't a faster guess. It's a steadier walk, with a crew beside you. Alpha is the edge you build. Walk is how you keep it.
