Full disclosures

What this is.
And what it isn't.

alphawalk is a companion for the long walk. Not a broker, not an adviser, not a fund. These notes spell out what we do, what we don't do, and what you keep deciding.

Last updated · 16 may 2026 · hello@alphawalk.ai
01 · not advice

Information, not advice.

alphawalk is an information, research, and learning tool. Nothing the crew writes, says, voices, or shows is a recommendation to buy, sell, hold, or size any security, derivative, token, or other financial instrument.

alphawalk is not a broker-dealer, investment adviser, financial planner, or fiduciary. We are software for research and trade-craft.

02 · no guarantees

No promises about the future.

Markets carry real and substantial risk of loss. Past performance — of any strategy, pattern, setup, or market — does not indicate future results. No claim is made that using alphawalk will produce profits or avoid losses.

Many people who trade actively lose money. Use of the product at any size, with any frequency, should reflect that reality.

03 · you decide

Alpha never touches the trigger.

Every decision to open, size, hold, or close a position is yours alone. Alpha and the crew read, reason, surface context, and push back — they do not place orders, custody assets, or move money on your behalf.

You should consider your own objectives, experience, risk tolerance, and financial situation — and, where appropriate, consult a licensed professional — before acting on any information from alphawalk or any other source.

04 · paper only

Paper trades, during beta.

During beta, alphawalk operates in paper-only mode. No real positions are opened, no funds are moved, and no broker integration is live. Any P&L, hit rate, or pattern signal reflects simulated activity for learning and review.

When live trading is introduced, opt-in will be explicit, with separate terms covering execution and custody handled by regulated third parties.

05 · data

Your conversations, your data.

alphawalk stores the minimum information needed to run your crew: preferences, watchlist, interaction history, and the messages we exchange to coach the model. We do not sell, share, or repackage walker data.

Conversations are delivered through third-party messengers (Telegram, WhatsApp). Their security and privacy practices govern the transport layer. End-to-end encryption is in place on every message channel we control. You can delete your history at any time; we wipe ours alongside on a 30-day cycle.

SOC 2 Type II audit is in progress · Q3 2026. Data residency available in US, EU, and SG.

06 · sources

Every call cites its source.

The crew reads market data (LSEG, Polygon, IEX), filings (SEC EDGAR, HKEX, SGX), macro indicators (FRED, Trading Economics, BIS), newsflow (Reuters, Bloomberg wires, curated), and options data (OCC chain, CME term structure).

Third-party data is subject to those providers' licensing and accuracy. We surface the receipt with every signal; you can trace any claim back to its source.

07 · coverage

Not available everywhere.

alphawalk is not available in every jurisdiction. Some features may be limited or unavailable depending on your location and applicable regulation. If you are unsure whether use is permitted where you are, contact us before starting.

For questions about coverage, data handling, or anything in these disclosures, write to hello@alphawalk.ai.
